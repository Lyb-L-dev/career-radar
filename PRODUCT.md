# Career Radar

Career Radar is a local, single-user career application for a 2026 graduate. It collects public company jobs and separately identified BOSS leads, preserves full JD text, compares documented candidate facts, and supports preparation of application materials.

The growth feature turns a user-selected group of AI application and Agent jobs into explicit requirements, independently assessed skills, daily learning and Career Radar project tasks, and spaced active recall. Its success is observable capability evidence and traceable gaps, not time spent studying.

The growth surface is an operating tool used daily: show a small next action, a readable prerequisite map, evidence behind every capability claim, and a clear path back to the job requirement. Existing FastAPI, React, SQLite, light neutral surfaces, blue actions, system typography, and Chinese labels are the visual and technical authority.

Users submit text, code, project explanations, and their own run records. The first version reviews those records but does not execute code. Resume facts, self-reported skills, independent answers, assisted practice, and project reviews have distinct provenance. Model judgments are grounded in quoted inputs and do not imply hiring probability.
